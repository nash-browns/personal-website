## The Trailhead
It can get tight out there. This isn't a huge touristy place, but the trailhead is small. You can see the trailhead circled below in red. I have seen more and more people parking in the blue box. However, I am not 100% sure if it is allowed. 

<CenteredImage image={trailheadMarkup} altText={'Golden View Trailhead parking circled in red with overflow parking marked in blue'} title={'The Trailhead'} width={1000} height={1000} />

## The Approach
There is no approach. Well, I guess there could be a small approach if you started further down Honey Bear Lane (blue box). From the main trailhead, you start heading up the ridge right from the parking lot.

## The Ridge
It's a fun one, and an easy run. You can cruise up the ridge to the obvious next incline.
<CenteredImage image={mchughRidgeDrawing} altText={'Route drawing up the ridge toward the summit of McHugh Peak'} title={'The Ridge'} width={1000} height={1000} />


## The Top
It looks technical, but it is easy to pick your way up. Maybe not for small kids? I can't really be sure about that. This is the path that I took, but obviously there are other routes — use judgment! The view from the saddle at the base of the thumb is the same!
<CenteredImage image={mchughTopDrawing} altText={'Route drawing of the path up the summit block of McHugh Peak'} title={'The Top'} width={1000} height={1000} />


I was able to see some wildlife while I was out there. Bear Valley always seems wild. I love it out here. Can you even see that ptarmigan? I won't lie, I almost ran right by it. The goats are just hanging around the area this summer. A blessing of the highest order!
