## Flow
Check the flow before you go! I did not. I could not find anything on the internet recommending a minimum flow for rafting. Here were the stats when I went:

* Skunk Creek: 33.2 CFS                                           -- Considered Average
* Big Sioux River at Sioux Falls: 43.9 CFS                        -- Considered Much Below Average
* Big Sioux River at North Cliff Ave at Sioux Falls, SD: 398 CFS  -- Considered Below Average

The above numbers were not high enough. There were some portions that needed to be walked and other portions that required lots of dragging on the bottom. I would consider the above numbers the minimum in order to have some kind of a good time!

You can check the current flow [here][2]!

## Putting In
I put in at Elmwood Park. I had never been to Elmwood Park and it was easy enough to find a place on the bank to launch from. I would say this is the case with this entire portion of the river. If you walked on top of the levee at any given point, I'm sure you would see a decent place to launch!

## Elmwood -> Madison Street
Not much water. There was a significant amount of dragging on the bottom. I had to get out of the boat to cross the riprap under the Madison Street Bridge.

## Zoo -> Confluence with Skunk Creek
Starting below the rocks was the worst water on the river. There was hardly any and I did not even attempt to launch. I ended up almost calling the trip at this point but I decided to walk down to Skunk Creek to see if there was more water. It turns out there was! I walked across the below bridge and down to the confluence to launch!
<CenteredImage image={bridgeToSkunk} altText={'Bridge to Skunk Creek'} title={'Bridge to Skunk Creek'} width={1000} height={500} />

## Skunk Creek -> Farm Field Park
The water came and went. There was still a significant amount of dragging. The closer to Farm Field Park I got the better the water. There were a few small rapids on this section. If that's not your thing I would skip!

## Farm Field Park -> Pasley Park
Probably the best water of the trip. There is also a fun little rapid at Tuthill Park. Some asshole decided to park his plane in the woods. Not sure what that is all about.

## Wildlife
<TwoColumn>
<CenteredImage image={mussle} altText={'Big Mussel'} title={'Big Mussel'} width={500} height={500} />
I was able to see a ton of waterfowl: birds, ducks, geese sitting on eggs, and Sandhill cranes. I was also able to see turtles and a couple of deer. The most exciting thing was a river otter. Am I sure that I saw this? No. Did I get a picture? No. However, I am pretty sure this is what I saw! Big mussels were also spotted.

## Farm Park Put-in
There is a nice kayak launch at this park. Honestly, Farm Park to Falls Park would be the most fun section of the river and is what I would recommend.
