### Icing And Regulator Failures
There was a night in Wyoming that my heater quit. I woke up in the cold and was pretty confused because:
1. I had just filled the propane tank that day
2. It wasn't that cold, ~30 degrees

When I got out of the camper it had snowed about 10" and all the lines and regulators had about 1/8 of an inch of ice on them. I assume this is why the propane stopped flowing. It also could have been that the regulator stopped working. This icing has only happened twice this winter. I think it takes a combo of snow, warm enough temps where the snow will melt on the roof, and a slight angle of the roof towards the propane tank. With all the factors in place, snow from the roof melts, drips on the regulator and lines, then refreezes causing an issue. Unfortunately, I was in a mood that morning and did not take any pictures, but the situation did happen again after I had implemented the fix. Here are some pictures to get an idea of the issue.

<ThreeCenteredImages image={[heaterIcicle3, heaterIcicle6, heaterIcicle5]} altText={['Ice on the propane regulator', 'Ice on the heater lines', 'Iced propane regulator']} titles={['Iced Regulator','Iced Lines', 'Iced Regulator']} width={300} />

The fix is to use water pipe insulation on the hoses and a faucet cover on the regulator. The second time there was icing on the lines and hoses was in South Dakota and the fix worked! Another benefit is that the faucet protector keeps the regulators from failing. I went through 3 regulators, but after using the faucet cover, I have had no issues. All three regulators that I used had a warning not to expose them to the elements printed on the side.
