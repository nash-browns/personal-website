## The Approach
You just head up Powerline Pass until you get to the bike rack. Then turn down the Hidden Lake Trail and follow it to the back of the valley!

## The Saddle and Peak
While going up the valley, it really looks like the peak would be on the south side of the ridge, but that is not the case!
